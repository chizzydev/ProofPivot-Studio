## 9.1 Nested-scroll / “scroll prison” problem

The Judge Door currently contains:

- the parent page scroll;
- Reader One iframe scroll;
- Reader Two iframe scroll.

The two readers must be navigated independently.

This creates a high-friction interaction:

```text
scroll parent
→ enter left iframe
→ scroll left
→ leave left
→ enter right iframe
→ scroll right
→ leave right
→ scroll parent again
```

- comparison is harder than it should be;
- the two panes drift out of visual alignment;
- important content appears at different vertical positions;
- the user repeatedly loses the global story;
